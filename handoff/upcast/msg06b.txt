prep(06b-field-method-name-clash): rename fields that share a name with a method

A Go struct cannot have a field and a method of the same name, and with
embedding a field named like a member of a supertype is ambiguous.

Rule (found and applied by tools/prep/06_find_name_clashes/Scan.java, a
javac-API scanner with attribution, run over java/src and java/test
together): a field F of class C, static or not, is renamed when
  * C declares a method named F, or
  * a supertype of C has a method named F (any method of a supertype in the
    sources; inherited methods of library supertypes, e.g. Enum.name()), or
  * a subtype of C declares a method named F, or
  * a supertype of C has a field named F.
Outer classes do not count. Record components and enum constants are exempt.
The method is never renamed. The one scheme: field `foo` -> `fooField`
(a constant `FOO_BAR` -> `FOO_BAR_FIELD`). The rewriter renames the
declaration and every identifier / member select that javac resolves to the
field symbol; parameters, locals, strings and comments are untouched, and no
line is re-wrapped.

Counts: 208 fields at 982 sites in 98 files.
  java/src (non-testing): 164 clash with a method (121 hand-written, 43 in
    the builders that 06a inlined) + 12 that only hide a supertype field
  java/src */testing/:    2 clash with a method
  java/test:              25 clash with a method (7 of them in 06a
    builders) + 5 that only hide a supertype field
The scanner reports 0 afterwards.

Non-enum Serializable classes whose serialized field names change
(serialVersionUID left alone): BasicBlock, ByPathWarningsGuard,
DiagnosticGroupWarningsGuard, DiagnosticType, TypedScope,
TranspilationException, CheckTypeSummaryWarningsGuard, HamtPMap,
NonJSDocComment.
